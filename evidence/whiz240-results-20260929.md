## What changed and what the experiment establishes

The total request deadline changed from 120 to 240 seconds, with the socket timeout changing from 110 to 230 seconds for all players. The 8,192-token cap, exact models, provider routes, prompts, effort settings, seed, rules, and cumulative $3/account pilot bound stayed fixed. Frontier resumed at action 100; mainstream began from its initial deal. The continuation added 124 actions: 94 model choices and 30 forced single-option moves.

The previously held Qwen decision returned in **81.495 seconds**, using **3,205 output tokens**, with a valid choice and normal stop. This was the slowest request in the new run. No request exceeded the old 120-second deadline, so successful completion does **not** isolate the earlier timeout's cause or establish that a longer deadline was necessary. Provider-side enforcement of the requested token limit remains unproven. No prior failure is erased or reclassified as a success.

Frontier completed at 01:09:25 UTC; mainstream at 01:24:49 UTC on September 29, 2026 (September 28 HST). The live step took about 17 minutes 45 seconds, including the retained OpenRouter cooldown.
