Your objective is to maximize your partnership’s probability of winning the game, not your individual trick count. Use the current bids, tricks, score, and bags to decide whether winning or avoiding a trick helps your team. Preserve your own live nil and protect your partner’s live nil while pursuing the partnership’s positive contract. When neither partner is nil, your tricks contribute to the same team contract. Consider setting opponents and bag risk in light of the overall game position. Once a nil has failed, its penalty is already incurred; adapt to the remaining objectives.
